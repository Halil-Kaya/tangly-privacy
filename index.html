Tangly – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Tangly ("the game") is made by Halil Kaya ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts of its own and no chat, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (solved shapes, stars, best times, coins, hints) and your settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, short full-screen ads between shapes, and optional rewarded ads for a free hint. Google may use your device's advertising identifier and collect usage and diagnostic data to show and measure ads. On iPhone we ask for your permission before tracking; if you say no, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent first. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Apple, RevenueCat)

You can buy the Starter Pack and coin packs. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. The game uses RevenueCat to check which purchases you own; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and basic device information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Leaderboards (Apple Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best solving times are sent to Apple and shown on leaderboards with your Game Center name. You can sign out or change this in your iPhone's Settings under Game Center.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • On iPhone, allow or stop tracking under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buy the Starter Pack to stop the banner and full-screen ads; optional rewarded ads stay available.
  • Delete the game to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
