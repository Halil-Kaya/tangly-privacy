Common questions

How do I play? Drag the wooden pieces onto the shape. Tap a piece to turn it. The shape is solved when every part of it is covered.

What are the hints? Angle fixes one piece's angle, Place shows where a piece goes and All Angles fixes every piece's angle. You can win them on the daily spin or buy them with coins.

I bought the Starter Pack on another device. Open the shop, go to Packs and tap Restore purchases.

Where is my progress? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game deletes it.
